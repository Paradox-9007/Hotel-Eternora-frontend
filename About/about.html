ABOUT US

Hotel ETERNORA

At Eternora, we believe that true luxury is not measured by square footage but by the depth of experience. Nestled in the heart of the world's most enchanting destinations, Eternora redefines hospitality with a philosophy rooted in connection, comfort, and curated elegance.

With our headquarters in Thailand, Eternora has expanded its presence across Southeast Asia and Taiwan, bringing our signature hospitality to some of the region’s most vibrant destinations. We proudly operate four hotels in Thailand, one in Myanmar, two in Indonesia, two in Malaysia, three in Singapore, and two in Taiwan. Every location is uniquely designed to reflect the spirit of its surroundings while maintaining the signature Eternora experience—personalized service, serene ambiance, and unforgettable moments.

Each Eternora property is designed to provide a sanctuary of comfort and tranquility, where every detail is thoughtfully curated to enhance your stay. Whether you seek a peaceful retreat, a cultural escape, or an inspiring workspace, Eternora ensures an experience where soul always comes before space.

Introduction and Homepage

Upon accessing our website, users are greeted by the homepage, which features a navigation bar at the top. This introductory page provides an overview of our platform, highlighting its primary function of offering comprehensive analysis dashboards specifically designed for hotel managers.

The homepage also includes a mini news carousel section extracted from the news page, ensuring that users are well-informed about relevant industry developments. Only the top 9 news from the news page are presented on the homepage, and by clicking on "Read More," users are directed to the designated news article.

Scrolling further down, there are two directories—one leading directly to the dashboard page and the other to the data source page. Each directory provides a brief overview of what users can expect to find on these pages.

Navigation Overview

The navigation bar is a central component of our website, providing easy access to various features. It includes options for "Home," "Dashboards," "Data Source," "News," and "User Guide." These options allow users to navigate seamlessly between different sections of the website.

In addition to the core features, our website also offers customization options to enhance user experience. Users can switch between light and dark modes via the navigation bar and change languages (English, Burmese, Malay, Indo, Chinese, Thai) to suit their preferences.

To further improve navigation, a "scroll to top" button is conveniently located at the bottom-right corner of every page, allowing users to return to the top effortlessly.

Dashboards

The "Dashboards" option in the navigation bar is a key feature of our website. Clicking on this option presents users with a sidebar that allows them to select from nine distinct analysis dashboards. These dashboards provide insights tailored to hotel managers, helping them make strategic decisions.

Users can navigate through the different analysis tabs in the sidebar using the up and down arrows or the W and S keys, ensuring an efficient and seamless browsing experience.You also have the option to hide the side analysis navigation bar for a clearer view of the dashboards. This option is available via the button at the bottom of the sidebar. Even when the sidebar is hidden, a small bar with 9 icons will remain visible, allowing you to seamlessly navigate between dashboards either with the cursor or the keyboard shortcuts.

Below each set of nine dashboards, you will find the AI Report and Forecast section, which provides a detailed analysis of the displayed data. By default, the system generates an initial report; however, you also have the option to request a customized report. This can be done either by typing your request or using the speech-to-text feature with built-in voice recognition, available in five languages supported on our website. Once your request is submitted, a new report will be generated. Additionally, there is a text-to-speech feature that allows the report to be read aloud for convenience.

Data Source

The "Data Source" option provides valuable information essential for utilizing our analysis dashboards.

Upon clicking this option, users are presented with a calendar and data tables. The calendar includes holidays for 2024, 2025, and 2026 for several countries, including Thailand, Myanmar, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, and Taiwan, assisting hotel managers in planning for seasonal fluctuations.

The tables provide crucial information on bookings and clients, offering insights into customer behavior and trends, which are essential for making informed decisions.

News

The "News" section of our website offers a comprehensive range of articles that cover a variety of topics within the travel industry, including Travel Trends and Focus, Travel Technology, and updates from leading Travel Associations. By simply clicking on the "News" option in the navigation bar, users are granted easy access to a carefully curated selection of the latest industry-related news articles, ensuring they stay informed about current developments and trends.

Our news content is sourced in real-time through an API integration with Travel and Tour World, providing live updates and guaranteeing that the articles you see are always fresh and up to date. Each category in the dropdown menu features 10 articles, specifically selected to cover key topics within that area. This ensures that users can easily find the most relevant and timely information within their area of interest.

Managers can leverage these articles to stay ahead of emerging trends, gaining valuable insights that can drive strategic improvements and innovations in their own operations. By tracking industry movements, they can make informed decisions to enhance services and adapt to the evolving travel landscape.

The Eternora Analysis website is a collaborative creation by Team Groove, a team of four dedicated students from Rangsit International College's Information and Communications Technology program. Every aspect—from frontend and backend development to data visualization and prompt engineering—has been a collective effort, rather than segmented among individuals. The team meticulously followed the full cycle of static website implementation, including usability testing, roadmap development, and strategic action planning. With a commitment to continuous improvement, Team Groove aspires to enhance Eternora and its hotel's analysis, ensuring ever-evolving insights and a seamless digital experience.
